**AiRCast-SR** is a generative atmospheric super-resolution framework that translates coarse-resolution (0.25°) outputs from the GraphCast global weather model into kilometer-scale (1 km) gridded analyses over the contiguous United States (CONUS). Built on a 3-D UNet conditioned with a Latent Consistency Model (LCM) scheduler, AiRCast-SR produces high-fidelity multi-variable predictions at substantially reduced inference cost compared to conventional diffusion models.
